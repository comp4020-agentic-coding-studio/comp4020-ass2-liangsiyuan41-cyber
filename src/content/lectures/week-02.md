The complete pre-shot setup, taught in order — the routine every later
week's stroke depends on. See [Week 2](/sessions/week-02/) for the full
session.

## Outline

- Stance — standing behind the cue ball and reading the shot line before
  entering the stance.
- Shot-line entry — aligning the feet and cueing arm with the shot line.
- Body alignment — lowering into a stable stance with the head and chin
  aligned to the cue.
- Bridge — a stable, guiding front hand, not a gripping one.
- Relaxed grip — a relaxed hand and wrist, so the cue can move naturally
  through it.
- The full pre-shot routine, repeated in order, from observing the shot line
  to warm-up strokes.
