## The brief

> Given a table layout, identify the shot you would play, the contact point
> that makes it, and one safe alternative if it fails.

This assessment tests reading, not potting. You are marked on whether your
reasoning about angles and outcomes is sound, not on whether the ball goes in.

## What you submit

A short written or recorded walkthrough of the table set for the assessment,
covering the shot you would play and why.
