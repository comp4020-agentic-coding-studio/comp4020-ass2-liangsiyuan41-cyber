## The brief

> Given a table layout, play a sequence of shots that clears it in the fewest
> visits, explaining before each shot how it sets up the next.

This draws together the whole course: reading the table, controlling the cue
ball, and planning more than one shot ahead.

## What you submit

Demonstrated live at the table, with a short stated plan before play and a
brief explanation of the reasoning afterwards.
