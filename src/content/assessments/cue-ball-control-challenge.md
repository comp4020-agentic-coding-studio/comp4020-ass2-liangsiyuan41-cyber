## The brief

> Play a short set of shots called by the assessor, each specifying not just
> the object ball but where the cue ball should finish.

Potting is assumed at this stage — the mark is for control of the cue ball
afterwards.

## What you submit

Demonstrated live at the table during a practical session; no written
submission.
