## The table at a glance

Before anything technical, get comfortable naming what you're looking at.
Every shot from here on starts by identifying these four things: the cue
ball, the object ball, the pocket you're aiming for, and the rail.

## A note on the rules

Exact competition rules vary between pool rulesets and venues. For this
beginner course, we use a simple, consistent set of turn-taking and foul
rules — enough to play safely and fairly together. This is a starting
point for learning the game, not a claim about official or competition
regulations.

## Think it through

- Which pocket is the most natural target for the object ball above?
- Which of two shots shown to you is obviously legal, and which is obviously illegal, under the course rules?
- What should you check before taking any shot at all?
