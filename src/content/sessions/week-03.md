## Common errors to check for

- Changing foot position after lowering into the stance.
- Squeezing the grip during delivery.
- Moving the upper arm excessively.
- Twisting the wrist.
- Lifting the body during impact.
- Steering the cue sideways.
- Stopping the cue immediately after contact.
- Repeatedly missing to the same side.

Strike the cue ball straight toward a cushion. If the delivery is
reasonably straight and the contact is appropriate, the cue ball should
rebound broadly back along the same line, as on the left. Drift to either
side, as on the right, points to one of the errors listed above. This is a
diagnostic check for consistency, not a test of perfection.

## Keep a simple practice log

A simple way to notice your own patterns: play 10 straight shots, in sets
of 3, and record the result of each as one of:

- Made
- Missed left
- Missed right

This isn't a graded score. It's a way to notice whether your misses cluster
to one side — which usually points back to one of the setup or stroke
errors above, rather than bad luck.
