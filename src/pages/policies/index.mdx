# Policies

## Attendance & Table Participation

Attendance & Table Participation is worth 10% of the final grade (see
[Assessments](/assessments/)). There are 12 weekly participation
opportunities; your best 10 results are counted, with each counted week
worth 1%. Your lowest two results are automatically dropped. This means
turning up to practical sessions and taking part in table time, not just
being present in the room.

## Weekly Online Quizzes

Weekly Online Quizzes are worth 10% of the final grade. There are 12 weekly
quizzes; your best 10 quiz results are counted, with each counted quiz worth
1%. Your lowest two quiz results are automatically dropped. Complete the
quiz for each week — linked from that week's page — as part of working
through the week's material.

## Assessment Submission

Follow the submission instructions and deadline stated on each assessment's
own page (see [Assessments](/assessments/)). Read the brief before you start,
and submit by the stated deadline.

## Safety & Table Conduct

- Handle cues and balls carefully at all times.
- Never swing a cue around other students.
- Keep the area around the table clear.
- Wait until the table is completely clear before taking a shot.
- Respect the equipment and share practice time with the rest of the group.

## Respectful Behaviour

Give and receive feedback constructively, take turns fairly, and play in a
way that respects other students and their time at the table.

## Missed Classes

If you miss a class, review that week's page and the lecture materials where
available, then practise the missed skills before the next session.

## Academic Integrity

Complete graded quizzes and assessments using your own work and
understanding.

## Course Materials

The course website — weekly pages, diagrams and lecture slides — is provided
for enrolled students' own learning.
