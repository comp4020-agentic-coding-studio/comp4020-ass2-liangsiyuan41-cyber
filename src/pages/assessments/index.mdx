# Assessments

Five components make up the final grade. The three with a submission or a
practical test each have their own brief below; attendance and quizzes run
every week and are described here.

| Component | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Attendance & Table Participation | 10% |
| Weekly Online Quizzes | 10% |
| Assessment 1: Reading the Table | 20% |
| Assessment 2: Cue Ball Control Challenge | 25% |
| Final: Three Shots Ahead Challenge | 35% |

**Attendance & Table Participation (10%)** — turning up to practical sessions
and engaging with table time, not just lecture attendance.

**Weekly Online Quizzes (10%)** — a short online quiz most weeks, checking the
rules and concepts covered so far.
