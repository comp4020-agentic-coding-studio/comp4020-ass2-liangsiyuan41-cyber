# Course Overview

## What this course is

SLOP1677 is a 12-week beginner pool course built around a single idea: every
shot should prepare the next one. Rather than treating potting as the whole
game, the course treats each shot as a small decision inside a longer plan —
where the cue ball ends up matters as much as whether the object ball goes in.

## Learning philosophy

Skills are introduced in the order they're needed to plan further ahead. You
can't control position until you can read a table, and you can't plan three
shots ahead until you can control position — so the course builds one
capability on top of the last rather than covering topics in isolation.

## Who this course is for

Complete beginners and casual players. No prior competition experience or
formal coaching is assumed — only a willingness to spend time at the table.

## Learning outcomes

By the end of the course, students should be able to:

- read a table and identify sound shot options, not just the most obvious one
- control cue-ball speed and spin to reliably finish in a chosen position
- plan and execute a short sequence of shots where each sets up the next
- apply basic safety play and table strategy in a casual match

## How the twelve weeks build

The course moves through three stages, roughly four weeks each:

1. **See the Shot** — rules, aiming, contact, and cue-ball movement.
2. **Control the Shot** — stop, follow, draw, spin, rails, and positional
   control.
3. **Think Beyond the Shot** — position play, three-shot planning, safety,
   table reading, and match strategy.

Detailed week-by-week content is developed as the course runs; see
[Weeks](/sessions/) for the current structure of each session.
